# Georgia Bank Financial Review

An educational portfolio work sample by Dana Rabandiyar comparing JSC Bank of Georgia and JSC TBC Bank for FY2025. The review focuses on profitability, loan growth, customer funding, operating efficiency and regulatory capital using public disclosures.

## Deliverables

- [Excel workbook](analysis/Georgia_Bank_Review_2025.xlsx) — source inputs, ratio formulas, comparison charts and validation diagnostics.
- [Three-slide presentation](output/Financial_Review_2025.pdf) — scope, headline results and questions for further due diligence.

## 2025 headline ratios

Values below match the workbook's `Comparison` sheet, rounded to two decimal places.

| Net customer loan growth | 15.64% | 10.04% |

| Cost / income | 29.97% | 31.42% |

## Scope and methodology

Financial inputs use separate-bank IFRS statements in GEL thousands; capital uses bank-only NBG Basel III disclosures. ROA and ROE divide full-year after-tax profit by the average of 2024 and 2025 year-end assets or equity. Loan growth compares year-end net customer loans; Bank of Georgia factoring and finance leases are excluded. Cost/income uses operating expenses as a positive amount divided by pre-impairment operating income. CAR divides regulatory capital by risk-weighted assets/exposures.

The workbook traces `Raw_Data` inputs through `Ratio_Calculations` to `Comparison`. Its `Source_Register` records document URLs, page references and access dates.

## Limitations and preparation

Funding definitions differ: Bank of Georgia includes client deposits and notes, while TBC uses customer accounts. Two-point averages, accounting classifications and unequal asset-quality scopes limit comparability; no cross-bank asset-quality ranking is made. Country context is from July 2025 and needs updating for a current assessment. This historical review is not a credit rating or investment recommendation.

The workbook was adapted from an earlier AI-assisted version. A page-by-page source tie-out and Excel recalculation testing are not documented; cached results are not evidence of those checks.
